# Decisions

## desired_codebase_quality: high

Please indicate the desired quality of the codebase. Decided by openlanes init on 2026-11-03, written to `.openlanes.yml`.

a) high: the highest quality software design, modularity, low inter-module coupling, high intra-module cohesion, design patterns, events, immutability and functional programming, at the cost of slower delivery, more rounds per plan and high standards for accepting the review. SOLID classes and maximum reuse in OO code; the most sophisticated UI. A "review-passed" PR returns to developers. **(chosen)**

b) standard: a reasonable middle ground. Similar modularity and full test coverage, but less strict on SOLID, immutability and functional programming; likely fewer rounds and faster delivery. A "review-passed" PR returns to developers.

c) mvp: the fastest and least designed codebase, for MVPs and prototypes, not for software with paying customers. Cheapest in AI tokens, most likely to have bugs, and a less sophisticated UI. A "review-passed" PR is accepted, as it has no blocking issues.
